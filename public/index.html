Glossary

AI terms the class has taught the Academy so far.

Example terms
AI Agent
An AI system that works toward a goal over several steps, deciding for itself which tools to use and when it is done, instead of giving one answer to one prompt.
Artificial Intelligence
The broad field of building computer systems that can do tasks we usually associate with human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognising images or making decisions.
Context
Everything the model can see when it responds: your prompt, the conversation so far, and any documents or tool results included with it. The model only works with what is in its context, and that space is limited.
Human in the Loop
A way of working where a person reviews, approves or corrects an AI system's work at key steps, especially before actions that are hard to undo, instead of letting it run fully on its own.
Prompt
The text you send to an AI model to get a response: your instructions, plus any examples or background information you include with them.
Tokens
The small chunks of text a model reads and writes, often a whole word or part of a word. Context limits, usage limits and costs are all measured in tokens.
